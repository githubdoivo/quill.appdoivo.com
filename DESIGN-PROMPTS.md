# Journal (journal.appdoivo.com): ChatGPT design prompts

Goal: a quiet, personal notebook that feels like your own paper journal (warm, a little
dreamy, never cute or childish), equally at home on a laptop and a phone. It must not look
AI-generated or like a template. Attach the current screenshots (in ~/Downloads/journal-refs/:
`current-today-desktop.png`, `current-page-desktop.png`, `current-board-desktop.png`,
`current-today-phone.png`) to prompt 1.

Send all prompts in the same chat so the style stays the same.

---

## 1. Four directions on one board

Redesign this private journal web app (screenshots attached). Keep exactly this content on
the "Hoje" screen: the top bar ("Diário", menu Hoje, Páginas, Quadros, Livros, Pesquisar,
Definições, a moon icon, "EN"); the date as the title; a "Como Está?" row of five mood choices
(Péssimo, Em Baixo, Assim-Assim, Bem, Ótimo) plus "Sem Humor"; a big writing box "O que vai na
mente?", Etiquetas, Quando, "Adicionar Fotos", "Guardar"; the diary list grouped by day; and
on the right a "Calendário de Humor" (a month grid where each day is tinted by mood) and
"Neste Dia".

Show 4 different directions side by side on one board, each as a desktop screen, A to D:
A. Pressed flowers: cream paper, faded botanical edges, plum ink, serif headings.
B. Night notebook: deep ink-blue page, soft lilac light, stars of white ink, very calm.
C. Washi & film: off-white with taped photos, masking-tape accents, typewriter details.
D. Linen minimal: textured linen grey, one muted rose accent, lots of air, Scandinavian.

Rules: writing is the hero and reads beautifully (comfortable line length); moods use ONE
colour family from light to dark (not a rainbow, no emoji faces); no stock 3D, no logos or
brand names; the background is never one flat colour (paper, linen or soft gradient).

## 2. The chosen direction, desktop and phone

Make direction [LETTER] as two finished screens: desktop 1536×1024 and phone 1024×1536.
On the phone the menu is a row of small icons with labels under the top bar (nothing
pinned to the bottom edge); the calendar goes under the diary.

## 3. Pages, Boards and Books in the same style

Three screens on one board (desktop): (a) "Páginas": a note page titled "Viagem a Lisboa" with
a heading, two to-dos (one ticked), a quote and a small table; (b) "Quadros": a moodboard
"Cozinha" with four coloured notes and two photos placed freely; (c) "Livros": a burn book page
(photo, a name, handwritten-feeling words on lined paper) with page arrows.

## 4. Dark and light

The "Hoje" phone screen twice side by side: light and dark.

## 5. Textures and icons only (no text)

From the chosen design give me ONLY: (a) the page background texture (1536×1024, seamless,
no text, no UI); (b) a paper texture for notes and book pages (1024×1024, seamless); and (c) on
a plain pure #00ff00 green background, the six menu icons and an app icon as clean line icons,
spaced apart. No letters anywhere.
